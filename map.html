Guide

Drag to move · Pinch to zoom · Long press to add · Tap a marker to select

Long press empty map to add markers. Tap a marker to select it, then long press elsewhere to move it or tap × below the zoom controls to remove it. Tap empty map to deselect. Tap the crosshair above the zoom controls to locate yourself. Its pulsing circle has a fixed 25-metre radius and scales with zoom; it does not represent GPS accuracy.

Location is requested only when you tap 🎯. It is not saved or sent to our server. Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which receives requests for the area you view. Markers remain until this page reloads. Internet connection required.
