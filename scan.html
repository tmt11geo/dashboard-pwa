    Capture and processing tips

    Use a textured, stationary subject in good light. Move the phone itself a little sideways, rather than only turning it. Hold still for Capture view; wait for SAVED, then move a small step sideways with most of the previous view still visible. No movement threshold blocks the next capture. Depth takes time; up to 12 views per session.

    Save capture session before closing the page. It includes corrected camera images, relative depth and calibration, stays local, and can be reopened for another stitching attempt. Unplaced views are retained. Stitch tries nearby placed views in two passes; it does not perform global bundle adjustment or guarantee a complete object reconstruction. Build relief fuses only placed views, in arbitrary units.
