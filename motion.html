Try a short loop

  1. Open this page in Android Chrome and tap Start AR tracking.
  2. Allow AR access. Move gently until tracking begins.
  3. Move sideways about one metre, then return to the starting point. Rotate the phone to compare rotation with translation.
  4. Tap Stop, then Save trajectory to keep the pose data.

Coordinates are relative to the first tracked position, with Y pointing up. Axes retain the runtime’s local orientation, not north. The trail keeps the latest 3,000 samples at up to 10 per second. Reset origin clears it. Tracking gaps break the line; a reference-space reset clears it to avoid mixing coordinate systems.

This is a local motion demo, not GPS, a room mesh, or survey-grade measurement. Drift and relocalization jumps are possible. A missing or emulated position is never recorded as real tracking. The app does not upload camera images or pose data; Save downloads positions and orientations only. Your browser and AR service handle camera access.

Requires HTTPS, immersive AR and DOM overlay support. On Android, use an ARCore-supported device with Google Play Services for AR enabled. If installed-PWA or Brave support fails, open the same address in Chrome. Unsupported devices show an explanation, not a simulated trajectory.
