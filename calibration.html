1. Prepare the target

Open printable target ↗

Print in landscape at 100% / actual size. Keep the white border. Attach the sheet flat to a rigid board; avoid glare. Use bright, even light.

Hold the phone in landscape before starting. Keep the whole board visible. Move the board between captures; keep the phone and board still while capturing. Do not change zoom or camera.

What happens after calibration?

This page validates camera measurements for the next scanner test. It does not yet apply them to the existing experimental relief scanner. Save the session and share that file in our conversation for independent checking.

A pass means the measurements agree across two view groups. It is not proof of reconstruction accuracy. Camera, crop, orientation, zoom and focus must match when the calibration is used. If focus locking is unavailable, the result remains provisional.
